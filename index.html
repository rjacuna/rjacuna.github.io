Ricardo J. Acuña is a postdoctoral researcher in mathematics at 上海数学与交叉学科研究院 (SIMIS). He did his PhD at Washington University in St. Louis under Matt Kerr. He enjoys making mathematical visualizations, swimming, and playing chess.
